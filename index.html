Our Love Story

Nine years ago, on March 18, 2018, our journey began when the Groom made a simple yet life-changing decision to pursue the Bride. What started as a chance connection soon blossomed into a beautiful relationship built on love and support. Along this journey, we have been blessed by the love, guidance, and support of our families, relatives, and friends, including those who are no longer with us today. Though they cannot be here to celebrate this special day, their love and cherished memories remain forever in our hearts, and they will always be a treasured part of our life story.

Now, at 29 years old, we have grown together through every high and low, supporting one another as we pursued our dreams and built the lives we envisioned. We find ourselves in a season where our careers are flourishing, but above all, we cherish the life we have built side by side. We may be complete opposites—a pessimist (Bride) and an optimist (Groom)—but somehow, those differences became the very thing that brought us together and made us stronger. What began as two people crossing paths grew into a partnership filled with love, trust, and companionship, making us inseparable ever since. We are a simple couple with big dreams: to continue growing together, create beautiful memories, and embrace life's adventures hand in hand. After nine wonderful years of love, laughter, and unwavering support, we are excited to begin our next chapter as husband and wife.

Reminders

Please Arrive on Time

Kindly be seated at the church no later than 2:30 PM, 30 minutes before the ceremony begins. Please note that some pews are reserved for our Principal Sponsors and immediate family members.

For seating assistance, kindly coordinate with our wedding coordinator, Little Miss Planner.

Semi Unplugged Ceremony

We invite you to be fully present with us during our wedding ceremony. While we welcome a few photos and videos, we kindly ask that you keep phones and cameras to a minimum and avoid blocking the aisle or our professional photographers. We encourage you to put your devices away and simply enjoy this special moment with us.

Respect the Sacredness of the Ceremony

Please observe silence, refrain from moving around during the ceremony, and help us maintain a solemn and meaningful atmosphere.

After the Ceremony

Following the church ceremony, light refreshments and a photo booth will be available at Roy’s Hotel. Upon arrival, kindly check the seating board to locate your assigned table and seat. For seating assistance, kindly coordinate with our wedding coordinator. Thank you for celebrating this special day with us. We look forward to sharing these beautiful moments together.
